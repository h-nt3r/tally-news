Ask

Questions about today's stories, answered on this computer by an open model. Nothing leaves it.

The model reads the top story's headlines. About a minute on an older computer.

How this works
  1. Counting. Questions like "which stories did the left miss?" are answered by code counting the data, instantly. No model is involved.
  2. Matching. Other questions are matched to today's stories by shared words. If nothing matches, you get "That is not in today's coverage." If a matched story already has its summary, you get that at once.
  3. Writing. Only when you ask, or when a story has no summary yet, {model_name} reads the best story's headlines, grouped by side without outlet names, and writes a few sentences. It can be wrong, so every answer lists the stories it used.

It answers one question at a time and remembers nothing. Your questions are not logged or sent anywhere. Rules and instructions: config/chat_prompt.txt.
